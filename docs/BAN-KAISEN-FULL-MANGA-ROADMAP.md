# BAN KAISEN — Full Manga Saga Roadmap

**Project status:** In development  
**Volume 1:** Whispers from The Heart  
**Opening chapter:** Chapter 1 — The White Van  
**Final arc title:** The Unnecessary Incident  
**Final-arc creative description:** “Straight up BS in a kettle.”  
**Canon exclusion:** Divine Arc is not part of BAN KAISEN.

## Canon guardrails

- Keep established character names, ages and relationships consistent.
- Opening cast: Kira Shikimoru (8), Rengi (8), Hikari Tokito (8), Daigo Shikimoru (14; Kira's older brother), and Haruka (24; the white-van driver).
- Core systems: Kuro Essence, Domains shaped by beliefs and fears, and Ban Law.
- Arc 9 remains **Prohibited Law** (Hikari Tokito).
- Arc 10 remains **Tokito Arc**.
- The 999,999 figure is a maximum chapter-numbering/platform capacity target, not a prewritten chapter count.
- The manga is canon-only: no filler chapters. A chapter is published only after its manuscript, page art and lettering are complete and reviewed.
- The titles and summaries marked as proposed below are creative scaffolding, not previously established canon.

## Saga arc roadmap

### 1. The Quiet Before — proposed
Introduce Kira's everyday life, friendship with Rengi, Hikari's place in the group, and the subtle rules that govern their world. Establish the white-van journey and the emotional stakes before supernatural conflict takes over.

### 2. Black Ink District — proposed
A neighbourhood mystery leaves dark traces that cannot be washed away. The first visible signs of Kuro Essence connect ordinary incidents to a larger hidden system.

### 3. The Bell That Forgot — proposed
A school incident causes people to lose the same memory. Kira and his friends discover that memory, fear and belief can influence supernatural phenomena.

### 4. Broken Lunchbox — proposed
A seemingly small conflict exposes deeper family pressures and the danger of treating Kuro Essence as a shortcut. The group learns that power has personal costs.

### 5. Red Thread Tournament — proposed
A structured contest introduces rival fighters, competing philosophies and rules that cannot be broken without consequence. The tournament tests judgement as much as strength.

### 6. The Borrowed Face — proposed
An antagonist uses imitation and mistrust to fracture the group. The arc explores identity, recognition and the difference between appearance and intent.

### 7. Under the Empty Stadium — proposed
A disappearance leads beneath a familiar sports ground. The investigation reveals a hidden space where abandoned fears have taken form.

### 8. Kuro Essence Rising — proposed
The first major escalation forces the cast to confront the true cost and limits of Kuro Essence. Earlier clues converge and the story moves into a larger conflict.

### 9. Prohibited Law — established title
Hikari Tokito takes centre stage as a prohibited rule is tested. Preserve the established Hikari connection and develop the consequences without changing the arc's title.

### 10. Tokito Arc — established title
Explore the Tokito history and its consequences. Keep this distinct from Arc 9 while carrying forward the unresolved character and worldbuilding threads.

### 11. The Glass Shrine — proposed
A sealed shrine reflects the fears of those who enter. The group must distinguish what the shrine shows from what is actually true.

### 12. Seven Unwritten Rules — proposed
Seven unstable Domains expose patterns that were never formally recorded. The cast learns that knowing a rule is not the same as understanding why it exists.

### 13. The City That Sank Upward — proposed
A district experiences impossible spatial changes as belief and physical law collide. The arc expands the scale of the setting while keeping character choices central.

### 14. No One Leaves at Dawn — proposed
A night-long siege forces difficult rescue decisions. The consequences should carry forward rather than reset after the arc.

### 15. The Hollow Parade — proposed
A public celebration conceals a coordinated Kuro Essence outbreak. Familiar symbols become unsettling as the group uncovers who benefits from the chaos.

### 16. Ashes of the First Domain — proposed
The origin of Domains is investigated and an accepted historical account is challenged. Reveal answers carefully without invalidating earlier established rules.

### 17. The Ban Court — proposed
The principles behind Ban techniques are challenged in a public forum. The arc examines responsibility, restraint and who has authority to define a prohibition.

### 18. All Names Erased — proposed
An enemy attacks identity and collective memory. The group must preserve its bonds when names, records and recognition become unreliable.

### 19. The Last Ordinary Day — proposed
The cast faces the approaching end, settles promises and makes deliberate choices. This is the emotional preparation for the final conflict, not a filler pause.

### 20. The Unnecessary Incident — final arc
The final battle brings the saga's systems, relationships and consequences together. Its tone deliberately escalates into absurd, unpredictable chaos — the creator's shorthand is **“straight up BS in a kettle.”** That phrase is a description, not the arc title.

## Publication integrity

Do not display placeholder page panels as finished artwork. Do not count outlines, generated drafts or empty chapter slots as published chapters. Do not fabricate source manga chapters from third-party scraper metadata.
