# BAN KAISEN — Story Bible & Manga Production Roadmap

**Project:** Original manga for Streamivio  
**Canon rule:** Divine Arc is deleted and excluded. Do not restore it under another name.  
**Final arc title:** **The Unnecessary Incident**  
**Final-arc description:** “Straight up BS in a kettle.”  
**Status:** Development roadmap. Proposed arcs are creative additions, not previously established canon.

## Core established material

- Volume 1: **Whispers from The Heart**
- Chapter 1: **The White Van**
- Opening cast: Kira Shikimoru (8, 127 cm; quiet, kind and imaginative), Rengi (8), Hikari Tokito (8), Daigo Shikimoru (14; Kira's older brother), and Haruka (24; the white-van driver). Named white-van classmates: Kaito, Renjiro, Daichi, Haruki, Takeshi, Taiga, Kuroda, Ryota, Shinobu and Masato.
- Established concepts: Kuro Essence, Domains, and Ban Law.
- Arc 9: **Prohibited Law** — Hikari Tokito.
- Arc 10: **Tokito Arc**.
- The story is intended to be canon-only, with no filler. LLMs may assist with drafting and continuity; the five named image models are artwork tools, not story-LLMs.

## Full arc roadmap

Arcs 1–8 and 11–20 below are newly proposed creative development to complete the saga. They can be revised as the manuscript is written. Arcs 9 and 10 preserve the titles already established.

1. **The Quiet Before** — The ordinary school-day routines of Kira, Rengi and Hikari establish their friendships and family lives. Small disturbances hint that the world is not as ordinary as it seems.
2. **Black Ink District** — A neighbourhood mystery introduces Kuro Essence through strange marks, missing time and an incident no adult can explain.
3. **The Bell That Forgot** — A school bell triggers a memory anomaly. The children learn that fear and belief can shape a Domain.
4. **Broken Lunchbox** — A personal conflict becomes the first test of loyalty; a seemingly minor curse reveals how Ban Law can impose a cost.
5. **Red Thread Tournament** — A youth contest hides a test of control, judgment and restraint. Winning is less important than understanding the rules.
6. **The Borrowed Face** — An enemy imitates trusted people, forcing the group to distinguish memory, identity and truth.
7. **Under the Empty Stadium** — A disappearance leads beneath the city, where a dormant Domain has begun to spread.
8. **Kuro Essence Rising** — The first large-scale confrontation exposes the danger of using power without understanding its price.
9. **Prohibited Law** — Hikari Tokito confronts a forbidden rule and the consequences of crossing it.
10. **Tokito Arc** — The Tokito history, its inherited burdens and Hikari's place in it are explored.
11. **The Glass Shrine** — A sealed shrine reflects the fears of those who enter and tests whether the group can face its own illusions.
12. **Seven Unwritten Rules** — Seven unstable Domains reveal a hidden structure behind Domain formation and Ban techniques.
13. **The City That Sank Upward** — A district turns inside out as gravity, memory and collective belief collapse together.
14. **No One Leaves at Dawn** — A night-long siege forces the cast to choose between competing rescues and accept that every choice has consequences.
15. **The Hollow Parade** — A public festival becomes cover for a coordinated Kuro Essence outbreak.
16. **Ashes of the First Domain** — The origins of Domains are uncovered, including a founding deception that reshapes the conflict.
17. **The Ban Court** — The laws governing Ban techniques are challenged before those who enforce them.
18. **All Names Erased** — The enemy attacks identity itself. The group must preserve one another through memory, promises and action.
19. **The Last Ordinary Day** — Before the final conflict, the cast returns to ordinary places, resolves personal promises and prepares for what may not be reversible.
20. **The Unnecessary Incident** — The final battle. The central enemy attempts to collapse Kuro Essence, Domains and Ban Law into one impossible rule. The cast must combine everything learned across the saga; victory requires a meaningful cost and the final resolution of the series' central themes. The creator describes this final arc as “straight up BS in a kettle.”

## Chapter 1 production anchor

**The White Van** remains the opening chapter in Volume 1, **Whispers from The Heart**. The existing 40-page reader navigation is a layout scaffold only. Each page must be replaced with an approved page script and original illustration before it is marked complete.

## Artwork and writing pipeline

- Story and dialogue: drafted with LLM assistance, then checked against this story bible and approved as canon.
- Character continuity: maintain model sheets, ages, clothing, proportions, expressions and recurring props.
- Mini-artwork: one distinct original thumbnail for every volume and chapter.
- Full pages: panel layouts, inks, tones, lettering and final quality review.
- Artwork model options supplied by the creator: AnimagineXL 4.0; WAI Illustrious v14.0 (Mature); Prefect Pony XL v3; T-PonyNAI3; Pony Diffusion V6 XL.
- These are image-generation models. They are not the LLMs used to draft the story.
