- **Shinobu Kaneko:** Short auburn hair, hazel eyes, usually laughing; cheerful joker.
- **Masato Fujimura:** Dark grey hair, brown eyes, calm personality; often looking out the window; quiet observer.

# Chapter manuscript

## Page 1
The warm morning sun peeked through the curtains of the Shikimoru house, casting soft golden light across a tidy bedroom. Outside, birds chirped happily as cherry blossom petals drifted through the neighbourhood.

On the bedside table...

**BEEP! BEEP! BEEP!**

An alarm clock rang loudly.

Under a mountain of blankets, an eight-year-old boy groaned.

“...Mmm...”

Without opening his eyes, Kira reached out lazily.

His hand completely missed the alarm.

Instead, it knocked over a small stack of manga.

**THUD!**

Kira's eyes immediately shot open.

“...Oops.”

## Page 2
The bedroom door slid open.

Daigo leaned against the frame with a grin.

“Good morning, sleepyhead.”

Kira rubbed one eye.

“...Morning...”

Daigo looked at the clock.

“You've got twenty-five minutes.”

Kira blinked.

“...Until breakfast?”

Daigo laughed.

“Until the van gets here.”

Kira froze.

“...The VAN?! Since when did we use vans?”

He jumped out of bed so quickly that his blanket wrapped around his legs.

**THUMP!**

He landed face-first on the floor.

Daigo burst out laughing.

“You okay?”

“...I'm fine...”

“...The floor attacked me.”

## Page 3
A few minutes later...

The bathroom mirror was completely fogged.

Kira hurriedly brushed his teeth.

His toothbrush was moving so fast that toothpaste flew onto the mirror.

Daigo walked past.

“You missed a spot.”

Kira looked around.

“Where?”

“...Your entire face.”

Kira looked in the mirror.

There was toothpaste on his cheek.

“...Oh.”

Daigo handed him a towel.

“You'd forget your own head if it wasn't attached.”

## Page 4
The kitchen smelled wonderful.

Fresh toast.

Rice.

Miso soup.

Scrambled eggs.

Their mother placed breakfast on the table.

“Sit down before it gets cold.”

Kira rushed into his chair.

“I'm starving!”

Daigo sat opposite him.

“You say that every morning.”

“I AM starving every morning.”

Their mother smiled.

“Then eat.”

Kira picked up his toast.

It was still hot.

“Ow!”

Daigo laughed.

“Didn't you learn yesterday?”

“...No.”

## Page 5
Ten minutes later...

Kira hurried towards the front door while trying to zip up his backpack.

“Where's my maths book?”

Daigo raised an eyebrow.

“The one in your hand?”

Kira looked down.

“...Oh.”

“...Right.”

Daigo smiled.

“I worry about you sometimes.”

“You worry too much.”

“I probably don't.”

## Page 6
Outside...

The morning air was cool and fresh.

Children walked along the pavement towards school.

A familiar white van slowly turned onto the street.

It stopped neatly outside the Shikimoru house.

The driver's window rolled down.

A cheerful young woman smiled brightly.

“Good morning, Kira!”

Kira waved excitedly.

“Morning, Miss Haruka!”

Haruka smiled.

“Ready for another school day?”

Kira looked at his backpack.

“...I think so.”

Daigo walked outside.

“He remembered everything today.”

Haruka laughed.

“That's progress.”

## Page 7
The side door of the white van slid open.

Laughter immediately spilled out.

“Kira!”

Kaito Yamamoto waved dramatically.

“You actually made it on time!”

Kira climbed inside.

“Barely...”

Renjiro Takahashi checked an imaginary watch.

“Thirty-seven seconds to spare.”

Daichi Suzuki nodded seriously.

“A new personal best.”

Haruki Nakano couldn't stop laughing.

“He deserves a trophy.”

Kira scratched the back of his head.

“...You're all mean.”

Haruka smiled through the mirror.

“They're only teasing because they like you.”

## Page 8
The van slowly pulled away from the curb.

Kira looked around.

Almost every seat was occupied.

Takeshi Saito patted the empty seat beside him.

“Sit here.”

“Thanks.”

As Kira sat down...

Taiga Watanabe leaned over the seat.

“Wanna play football after school?”

“If Daigo says yes.”

“You always ask Daigo.”

“...He's my older brother.”

“Exactly.”

Everyone laughed.

## Page 9
Near the back...

Kuroda Juro was quietly reading a manga volume.

Ryota Kimura peeked over his shoulder.

“You've read that three times already.”

“It's still good.”

Shinobu Kaneko smiled.

“I think Kuroda would read anything with ninjas.”

“They're cool.”

Masato Fujimura looked out of the window.

“...Can someone be quiet for five minutes?”

Silence.

...

One second later—

Everyone started talking again.

Masato sighed.

“I tried.”

Even Haruka laughed.

## Page 10
The white van continued through the peaceful streets.

The children chatted happily about homework, games, football and what snacks they had brought for lunch.

Kira rested his chin against the window, watching the town pass by.

It was another ordinary morning.

Another ride with friends.

Another day waiting to begin.

Haruka glanced at the children through the rear-view mirror and smiled.

“I'm glad everyone's here.”

Kira smiled to himself.

“...Me too.”

The white van continued towards the school as the morning sun shone brightly overhead.

## Page 11
The white van continued through the quiet streets as the children chatted over one another.

Haruka chuckled while keeping her eyes on the road.

“One at a time! I can't understand anyone.”

Everyone stopped.

For exactly two seconds.

Then—

“I WAS TALKING FIRST!”

“No, I was!”

“You interrupted me!”

The van filled with laughter.

Haruka sighed dramatically.

“I should've known.”

## Page 12
Kaito leaned over Kira's seat.

“So... did you finish yesterday's maths homework?”

Kira blinked.

“...There was homework?”

The entire van went silent.

Renjiro slowly turned toward him.

“...You're joking.”

Kira smiled nervously.

“...Maybe.”

Daichi covered his face.

“We're doomed.”

Haruki laughed so hard he almost slipped off his seat.

## Page 13
Masato reached into his backpack.

“I brought the worksheet.”

He held it up.

Kira's eyes sparkled.

“Can I copy it?”

Renjiro immediately shook his head.

“No.”

Kaito folded his arms.

“You should've done it yourself.”

Kira lowered his head dramatically.

“...I've been betrayed.”

Haruka laughed.

“No copying in my van.”

Kira sighed.

“Even you?”

## Page 14
The van stopped beside another street.

The side door slid open.

A boy with neat silver hair stepped inside carrying a school bag.

“Good morning, Miss Haruka.”

Haruka smiled warmly.

“Good morning, Hikari.”

Hikari Tokito gave a polite nod.

“Morning.”

Some of the boys waved.

Others greeted him casually.

“Morning.”

“Hey, Hikari.”

“You're early today.”

Hikari smiled faintly.

“I woke up before my alarm.”

Haruki gasped.

“Is that even possible?”

## Page 15
Hikari quietly took the empty seat across from Kira.

The van started moving again.

For a few moments...

Everything became peaceful.

Kira glanced toward Hikari.

Hikari noticed.

“...What?”

Kira looked away quickly.

“Nothing.”

“...Sorry.”

Hikari simply smiled.

“It's alright.”

## Page 16
Taiga suddenly stood up slightly from his seat.

“Question!”

Haruka raised an eyebrow.

“I'm almost afraid to ask.”

“Who's faster?”

“Kira...”

“...or a turtle?”

The van exploded with laughter.

Kira crossed his arms.

“I'm faster than a turtle.”

Haruki grinned.

“Prove it.”

“I don't have a turtle.”

“Convenient.”

## Page 17
Ryota looked out the window.

“Hey!”

“Look!”

Everyone turned.

Outside...

A small dog chased a butterfly across a park.

“Awww!”

“So cute!”

Haruka smiled.

“It looks like he's having the best morning ever.”

Kira watched quietly.

“I wish every day was like this.”

Hikari looked out the same window.

“...Me too.”

## Page 18
The school came into view.

Haruka slowed the van.

“We're almost there.”

Immediately everyone started packing their bags.

Masato zipped his backpack.

Kaito stretched.

Renjiro yawned.

Haruki looked exhausted already.

“We haven't even started school...”

“I know...”

“I'm tired in advance.”

## Page 19
The white van stopped outside the front gate.

The side door opened.

One after another, the children climbed out.

“Bye, Miss Haruka!”

“See you later!”

“Drive safely!”

Haruka waved with a bright smile.

“Have fun today.”

“And remember...”

“No running in the hallways.”

Taiga immediately started running.

Haruka laughed.

“I knew he'd do that.”

## Page 20
Kira adjusted his backpack and looked around the school courtyard.

Students filled the grounds with laughter and conversation.

Suddenly—

“KIRAAAAAA!!”

A familiar voice echoed across the courtyard.

Rengi sprinted toward him at full speed, waving both arms in the air.

“There you are!”

Before Kira could react—

**THUD!**

Rengi crashed into him with a huge hug, sending both boys tumbling onto the grass.

The children nearby burst into laughter.

Kira smiled despite himself.

“Good morning, Rengi...”

Rengi grinned.

“Today's gonna be awesome!”

Kira laughed.

“...Yeah.”

“I think it will.”

## Page 21
The morning bell hadn't rung yet.

Students filled the school courtyard.

Some played tag.

Others chatted near the flower beds.

Rengi dusted himself off after tackling Kira.

**Rengi:** “Come on! Let's get to class before we're late!”

**Kira:** “You were the one who knocked me over...”

Rengi scratched the back of his head.

“...Details.”

Both laughed.

## Page 22
As they walked toward the school building, Kaito called from behind.

**Kaito:** “Hey! Slow down!”

Renjiro, Daichi, Haruki, Takeshi, Taiga, Kuroda, Ryota, Shinobu and Masato caught up with them.

Haruki grinned.

“I thought Rengi was going to break Kira's ribs.”

Rengi gasped dramatically.

“I would never!”

Kira smiled.

“I believe you...”

“...Mostly.”

Everyone laughed.

## Page 23
Inside the classroom.

The children placed their bags beside their desks.

The classroom buzzed with conversation.

A girl near the window watered the class plants.

Another student cleaned the blackboard.

Kira looked around.

“...It feels nice today.”

Rengi dropped into the seat beside him.

“It always does.”

## Page 24
Their teacher entered the classroom carrying a stack of books.

**Teacher:** “Good morning, everyone.”

The class stood together.

“Good morning, Sensei!”

“Please take your seats.”

Everyone sat down.

The teacher smiled.

“I hope everyone had a wonderful evening yesterday.”

Taiga raised his hand immediately.

“I scored two goals!”

The class chuckled.

## Page 25
The teacher began taking attendance.

“Kaito Yamamoto.”

“Here!”

“Renjiro Takahashi.”

“Present.”

“Daichi Suzuki.”

“Here.”

“Haruki Nakano.”

“Yep!”

“Takeshi Saito.”

“Here.”

“Taiga Watanabe.”

“Present.”

“Kuroda Juro.”

“Here.”

“Ryota Kimura.”

“Present.”

“Shinobu Kaneko.”

“Here!”

“Masato Fujimura.”

“Present.”

“Kira Shikimoru.”

“Here.”

“Rengi.”

“Present!”

The teacher smiled.

“Wonderful.”

## Page 26
The morning lesson passed peacefully.

Pencils scratched across notebooks.

The only sounds were turning pages and quiet whispers.

Kira carefully wrote each answer.

Rengi leaned over slightly.

“...What's number four?”

Kira whispered back.

“You weren't listening.”

“...No...”

“I know.”

## Page 27
**RING! RING!**

The recess bell echoed through the school.

Before the teacher could finish speaking—

Taiga was already halfway out the door.

“FOOTBALL!”

Haruki chased after him.

“Wait for me!”

Kaito laughed.

“They never change.”

## Page 28
Outside, the playground came alive.

Some students raced toward the football field.

Others climbed the jungle gym.

A few sat beneath the cherry trees reading books.

Kira and Rengi walked together across the playground.

“So...”

Rengi smiled.

“Football?”

Kira nodded.

“Football.”

## Page 29
The boys divided into two teams.

Taiga pointed confidently.

“Kira's on my team!”

Haruki folded his arms.

“No way.”

“We're taking him.”

Rengi stood beside Kira.

“Then I'm with Kira.”

Kaito laughed.

“Looks like we're choosing sides already.”

Even Masato smiled.

## Page 30
The football rolled into the centre of the field.

Taiga placed one foot on top of it.

“Ready?”

Everyone nodded.

Kira looked around at his friends.

Some were smiling.

Some looked determined.

Some were already arguing over the rules.

Kira couldn't help smiling.

“This is going to be fun.”

The referee's whistle blew.

**FWEEEEET!**

## Page 31
The whistle echoed across the playground.

**“Begin!”**

Taiga kicked the football forward.

“Let's go!”

The game immediately became chaotic.

Rengi sprinted after the ball.

“Pass! Pass!”

Haruki laughed.

“You've had it for two seconds!”

## Page 32
Kira chased after the ball as Kaito dribbled around Daichi.

“Too slow!”

“Hey!”

Daichi tried to catch him but tripped over his own feet.

**THUD!**

Everyone stopped.

“...”

Daichi slowly looked up.

“I'm okay...”

The boys burst into laughter.

Even Daichi laughed.

## Page 33
The ball rolled toward Kira.

“Here!”

Rengi called.

Kira stopped it with his foot.

He looked left.

Then right.

Taiga was completely open.

Kira smiled.

“I got you.”

He passed the ball perfectly.

Taiga kicked—

**GOAL!**

“YES!”

Taiga jumped into the air.

“Kira! That pass was awesome!”

Kira rubbed the back of his head.

“It wasn't that amazing...”

## Page 34
The lunch bell rang.

**RIIIIING!**

Haruki immediately dropped onto the grass.

“I'm exhausted...”

Kaito laughed.

“We played for twenty minutes.”

“Exactly.”

“I'm exhausted.”

Taiga shook his head.

“You're unbelievable.”

## Page 35
The boys sat beneath a large cherry blossom tree.

Lunch boxes opened one after another.

“Wow!”

Ryota stared at Kira's lunch.

“That looks amazing.”

Kira smiled.

“My mum made it.”

Haruki leaned over.

“Can I have one?”

“No.”

“Please?”

“...Maybe one.”

Haruki grinned.

“You're the best!”

## Page 36
Across the courtyard, Hikari sat quietly on a bench with a book.

Kira noticed him sitting alone.

He walked over.

“Hi, Hikari.”

Hikari looked up from his book.

“...Hello.”

“Mind if I sit here?”

Hikari nodded.

“I don't mind.”

Kira sat beside him.

For a while...

Neither of them spoke.

The silence wasn't awkward.

It was peaceful.

## Page 37
After a few moments...

Hikari closed his book.

“You like football.”

Kira smiled.

“Yeah.”

“You play well.”

“I mostly just pass.”

“Passing is important.”

Kira looked surprised.

“You think so?”

Hikari nodded.

“A good team isn't made of people who only score goals.”

Kira smiled.

“...I like that.”

## Page 38
The final school bell rang.

Students poured out of the classrooms.

Back at the front gate, Haruka was already waiting beside the white van.

She smiled as the children climbed aboard.

“How was everyone's day?”

“Great!”

“I'm tired!”

“I got full marks!”

“I forgot my homework...”

Haruka laughed.

“Sounds like a normal school day.”

## Page 39
The van made its way through town.

The afternoon sunlight streamed through the windows.

Some of the boys talked quietly.

Others stared outside.

A few had already fallen asleep.

Haruka glanced in the rear-view mirror.

She smiled warmly.

“You all worked hard today.”

Kira looked out the window.

“...I can't wait for tomorrow.”

Rengi grinned.

“Me neither!”

## Page 40
The van stopped in front of the Shikimoru house.

“See you tomorrow, everyone!”

“Bye, Miss Haruka!”

One by one, the children waved goodbye.

Kira stepped out and slung his backpack over his shoulder.

Daigo was waiting by the front gate.

“How was school?”

Kira smiled brightly.

“It was fun.”

As the brothers walked home together, the setting sun painted the sky orange.

Children laughed in the distance.

A gentle breeze carried cherry blossom petals through the air.
